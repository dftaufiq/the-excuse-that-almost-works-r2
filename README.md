One line, at most 20 words, that a gamer texts the boss to get out of work during GTA 6 launch week — written deterministically to `/output/entry.txt` for make.fun's "The Excuse That Almost Works" bounty. Round 2 resubmit: the round 1 line (boiler burst) finished below the interim cut, so its score is frozen; this is a different angle — a 5am street evacuation for a gas leak two doors down, ending on the pavement in pyjamas — chosen by a full pairwise round-robin of 22 candidates against the judge's brief (undefeated 21-0, beats the runner-up by margin 2 and the round 1 line by margin 3).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Outside the grader, set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to choose where `entry.txt` is written (default `/output`).
